Privacy

Privacy policy

Short, because we collect very little. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect

Two things, and nothing else.

1. What you send us through the project form

If you complete the form on Start a Project, we receive what you typed: your name, email address, company if you give one, and your description of the problem, systems, budget range and timeline.

We use it to reply to you about your project. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside Digital Galactica Labs, and we do not add you to a marketing list. If we are not the right fit we will say so, and that is the end of it.

Submissions are stored in a private Google Workspace account belonging to Digital Galactica Labs and are kept while a conversation is live, and for up to two years afterwards so we can pick up where we left off. Ask and we will delete yours sooner.

2. Anonymous usage measurement

We use Google Analytics to count page views and a small number of actions - which pages get read, which buttons get clicked, and whether the project form was started and completed. IP addresses are anonymised.

The contents of the form are never sent to analytics. We record that a submission happened, never what it said.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell repeat visits apart. Nothing on this site requires you to accept them, and blocking them does not break anything.

Who else sees it

Google, as the provider of the Workspace account where form submissions are stored and of the analytics we use. GitHub, which hosts this site and therefore handles the request that delivered this page. Neither is given your information for their own purposes.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it. We will, and we will confirm when it is done.

Email Taylor@DGL.dev and a person will answer.

Children

This is a business to business site. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page changes on the same day. Last updated August 2026.

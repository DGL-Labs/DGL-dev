How we work

Your idea, built right.

Transparency

You can see the code, project board, decisions and deployment history. You should know what is being built and where your money is going.

Integrity

If custom software is the wrong answer, we will tell you before you pay us to build it. If an existing product solves the problem better and cheaper, that matters.

Practical Engineering

We make technical decisions based on the problem, expected usage, operating cost, maintainability, and the people responsible for the software after launch.

Reliability

We operate our own production software. We know what happens when a deployment fails, an API changes, or a customer cannot log in outside business hours.

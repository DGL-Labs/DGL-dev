Fall refresh of the automated emails

21 automated flow emails across 12 flows get a fall look. Each one is shown below as it is live today and as it will look after the change. Nothing has been changed yet. Choose Approve or Request changes under each email, add notes where something should change, then submit at the bottom.

Internal review page. These are preview renders, not emails sent to customers. Personal discount codes show as “PREVIEWCODE”, and the “before” side shows raw template text such as {{ organization.name }}.

What changes

  • Fixed summer product picks become fall best sellers: knits, cardigans, long sleeves, denim and boots. Each email has its own mix.
  • Each email opens on a different fall product photo.
  • Top menu: SWIM becomes FALL and links to the Fall collection.
  • Welcome #3 and Repeat Customer #3: “Swim season is calling” becomes “Sweater season is here”, and the 10% button says “Shop Fall With 10% Off”.
  • Cross-sell emails show 2 boots, 1 cozy accessory and 1 piece of jewelry.

What stays the same

  • Offers and discount codes (WELCOME10, REPEAT10, CART10 and others).
  • When each email sends, who gets it, and how each flow is triggered.
  • Price Drop products, which show sale prices (a separate fix).
  • Birthday and review-request emails, which have nothing summery in them.
  • Old versions are kept, so any email can be switched back in one step.

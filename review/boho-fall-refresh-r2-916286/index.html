Fall refresh of the automated emails: round 2

21 automated flow emails across 12 flows get a fall look. Each one is shown below as it is live today and as it will look after the change. Nothing has been changed yet. Choose Approve or Request changes under each email, add notes where something should change, then submit at the bottom.

Round 2, after your first review. Previews are rendered the way customers receive them: with the real Boho Beach Hut name and address, a sample cart in the abandoned-cart emails, and a sample dropped product in Price Drop. Personal discount codes show as “PREVIEWCODE”. These are previews, not emails sent to customers.

Your round 1 notes and what we changed

Round 1 (Taylor Reasoner, 3 Oct 2026): see the round 1 page. 21 of 21 emails had changes requested.

You askedWhat we foundWhat changed
Social logos and links are missing at the bottom (all 21)True of the live emails today: 15 had no social links and 6 had plain text linksEvery email now ends with Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest icons that link to Boho’s pages

What changes

  • Fixed summer product picks become fall best sellers: knits, cardigans, long sleeves, denim and boots. Each email has its own mix.
  • Each email opens on a different fall product photo.
  • Top menu: SWIM becomes FALL and links to the Fall collection.
  • Welcome #3 and Repeat Customer #3: “Swim season is calling” becomes “Sweater season is here”, and the 10% button says “Shop Fall With 10% Off”.
  • Cross-sell emails show 2 boots, 1 cozy accessory and 1 piece of jewelry.
  • Every email now ends with Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest icons, then the Boho Beach Hut name and address and Unsubscribe.
  • Price Drop: its lower product grid becomes fall best sellers too.

What stays the same

  • Offers and discount codes (WELCOME10, REPEAT10, CART10 and others).
  • When each email sends, who gets it, and how each flow is triggered.
  • The Price Drop email still shows the product that dropped, with its old price, new price and savings, filled in when it sends.
  • Birthday and review-request emails, which have nothing summery in them.
  • Old versions are kept, so any email can be switched back in one step.

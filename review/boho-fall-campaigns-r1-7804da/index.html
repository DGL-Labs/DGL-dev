Fall campaign calendar, 10-05 to 11-21

18 scheduled email campaigns (3 with an SMS) are ready as drafts in Klaviyo. Each is shown below with when it sends, who gets it, its subject line and the email itself. Nothing is sent until it is approved and released. Choose Approve or Changes required under each campaign, add notes where something should change, then submit at the bottom.

Round 1, 3 Oct 2026. Each email is shown as a subscriber without personal picks sees it, with the real Boho Beach Hut name and address. Most subscribers get a personalized or rotating product grid drawn from the same fall products. These are previews; nothing has been sent.

How the calendar works

  • Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays go to subscribers engaged in the last 30 days.
  • Saturdays go to all engaged subscribers, with an SMS on the Saturday campaigns.
  • Emails use Klaviyo Smart Send Time; texts go at 12:30 PM local time.
  • Every campaign excludes the Hygiene Pool (bounced or long-unengaged addresses).
  • Only fall products: summer items are kept out of every product grid.

What approval means

  • Approve: the campaign is accepted as shown.
  • Changes required: tell us what to change in the notes; a revised version comes back for review.
  • Before each send, product picks are refreshed and every link is checked again.
  • No discounts are included in any of these campaigns.
